Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Data Controller

FitRival is operated from Denmark. We are the data controller responsible for your personal data. If you have questions about this policy, contact fitrival@hotmail.com.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following data:

  • Account information: email address and name.
  • Profile data: avatar, workout preferences, goals.
  • Workout data: exercise logs, duration, type, notes.
  • Social data: friend connections and activity interactions.
  • Device and app info: device type, OS, app version.
  • Usage and diagnostics: app interactions and crash data to improve reliability.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the FitRival service.
  • Consent: for optional features like push notifications (where applicable).
  • Legitimate interests: to improve the app and prevent fraud/abuse.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain FitRival.
  • Track workouts and calculate streaks.
  • Enable social features (friends, leaderboards) based on your privacy settings.
  • Send notifications about progress (if enabled).
  • Improve performance and security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data:

  • With other users: your profile and stats as controlled by your privacy settings.
  • With service providers: Supabase (database/authentication hosting) to operate the app.
  • When required by law.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored with our service providers. We use security measures such as:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Secure password hashing (passwords are not stored in plain text).
  • Access controls and database row-level security where applicable.

Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact fitrival@hotmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data unless retention is required by law.

Children’s Privacy

FitRival is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.
