Troubleshooting

  • App stuck on the logo / loading screen
    Force close the app, reopen. If it keeps happening, reinstall and try again.
  • Login issues
    Check your internet connection. Try resetting your password, then log in again.
  • Something looks broken
    Update to the latest version, then restart your phone.

Privacy

FitRival may request access to features like Camera, Contacts, Calendar, or Location only to enable app functionality. You can control permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
