Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

At FitRival, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Authentication credentials (encrypted)

Fitness Data

To provide our core features, we collect:

  • Workout Data: Type of exercise, duration, calories burned, date and time
  • Streak Information: Workout frequency and consistency
  • Challenge Progress: Your performance in challenges
  • Goals: Your weekly workout targets and rest days

Health App Integration

If you choose to connect Apple Health, we access:

  • Workout sessions and activity types
  • Exercise duration and intensity
  • Calories burned (if available)

Important: We only read this data - we never modify your health records without explicit permission. You can revoke access at any time through your device settings.

Social Features

When you interact with others, we collect:

  • Friend connections and requests
  • Challenge invitations and participation
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Comments and interactions (if applicable)

Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Crash reports and error logs (anonymized)
  • Push notification tokens (if you enable notifications)

Location Data

We do NOT collect or track your precise location. Any location-based features use only the data you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Provide Core Features

  • Track your workouts and streaks
  • Calculate challenge progress and rankings
  • Display leaderboards and friend activity
  • Sync data from health apps
  • Send you relevant notifications

Improve the App

  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Fix bugs and improve performance
  • Understand which features users find valuable
  • Develop new features based on user needs

Communication

  • Send push notifications about challenges, streaks, and friend activity (with your permission)
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Send important updates about the app or policy changes

Safety and Security

  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect user accounts and data

3. Information Sharing

With Other Users

The following information is visible to your friends:

  • Your name and profile photo
  • Your current streak and total workouts
  • Your challenge participation and progress
  • Your leaderboard rankings

You can control some privacy settings in the app.

With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third parties who help us operate the app:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication (data hosting)
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting (anonymized)
  • Apple/Google: Push notifications and app distribution

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties
  • Share your health data with insurance companies
  • Use your data for targeted advertising
  • Share your workout data with employers or other organizations

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes or government requests
  • Protect our rights and safety
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activity
  • Protect the safety of our users

4. Data Storage and Security

Where We Store Data

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase, which uses industry-standard security practices including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

How We Protect Your Data

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • All API communications are encrypted
  • Access to user data is restricted to essential personnel only
  • We regularly review and update our security practices

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your personal information is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for analytics
  • Backup systems may retain data for up to 90 days
  • Legal requirements may require us to retain certain data longer

5. Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have about you
  • Update: Modify your profile and preferences in the app
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data
  • Object: Opt out of certain data processing activities

Privacy Settings

You can control:

  • Who can see your profile and activity
  • Which notifications you receive
  • Health app data sync (enable/disable anytime)
  • Friend requests and connections

Opting Out

  • Push Notifications: Disable in app Settings or device settings
  • Health Sync: Revoke permissions in device settings
  • Analytics: Limited opt-out available in app Settings

6. Children's Privacy

FitRival is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

Users aged 13-17 should have parental consent before using the app.

7. International Users

FitRival is available globally. By using our app, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The FitRival app does not use cookies. Our website (this page) may use basic analytics to understand visitor patterns, but does not track individual users or use advertising cookies.

9. Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to third-party websites or services (like App Store/Play Store). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email (if provided)
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date above

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or shared
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at fitrival@hotmail.com

12. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you're in the European Economic Area, you have rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at fitrival@hotmail.com

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • Email: fitrival@hotmail.com
  • Support Page: zaenj.github.io/fitrival-support/support.html

Your Privacy Matters

We're committed to protecting your data and being transparent about how we use it. If you have any concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
