Support & FAQ

Get answers to common questions about FitRival

Getting Started

🚀 How do I create an account?
Download FitRival from the App Store or Google Play, open the app, and sign up with your email or use Apple/Google sign-in. It takes less than a minute!
📱 How do I sync my workouts from Apple Health?
Go to Settings → Health Sync and enable automatic syncing. FitRival will detect your workouts automatically and update your streaks and challenges. You'll need to grant permissions when prompted.
👥 How do I add friends?
Tap the Friends tab, then tap the "+" button. Search for friends by name or share your profile link. When someone accepts your request, you'll be able to see each other's progress and compete in challenges together.

Challenges

🏆 How do I create a challenge?
Go to the Challenges tab and tap the "+" button. Choose your challenge type (workouts, streak, or minutes), set a goal and end date, then invite friends. You can make challenges public, friends-only, or private.
✅ How do I join a challenge?
When a friend invites you to a challenge, it will appear in your Invites tab. Tap "Accept" to join. You can also browse and join public challenges in the Discover section.
🎯 What types of challenges can I create?
Workout Challenges: Complete a specific number of workouts
Streak Challenges: Maintain a workout streak for X days
Minutes Challenges: Accumulate total workout minutes

All challenges can have custom durations and goals.
🏅 How are challenge winners determined?
The participant who reaches the goal first wins! If multiple people reach the goal, the first person to hit the target wins. If no one reaches the goal by the end date, the participant with the most progress wins.
🔗 How do I share a challenge?
Open any challenge and tap the share icon. You can share via text, email, or social media. Friends without the app will be directed to download it, then they'll automatically join your challenge!

Streaks & Progress

🔥 How do streaks work?
Your streak increases each day you complete at least one workout. Miss a day and your streak resets to zero. You can set rest days in Settings to maintain your streak on scheduled off days.
📊 What counts as a workout?
Any activity tracked through Apple Health or manually logged in FitRival counts as a workout. This includes running, cycling, strength training, yoga, and more.
📅 Can I set rest days?
Yes! Go to Settings and select your rest days. Your streak won't break on these days, and they won't count against your weekly goals.

Account & Settings

🔔 How do I manage notifications?
Go to Settings → Notifications to customize what alerts you receive. You can toggle streak reminders, friend activity, challenge updates, and more.
🌙 Is there a dark mode?
Yes! FitRival automatically follows your device's system settings. You can also manually toggle dark mode in Settings → Appearance.
🔒 How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Please note this action is permanent and will delete all your data, including streaks, challenges, and trophies.

Troubleshooting

❓ My workouts aren't syncing from Apple Health
1. Make sure Health Sync is enabled in Settings
2. Check that FitRival has permissions to read workout data in the Health app
3. Try manually syncing by pulling down on the home screen
4. If issues persist, contact support
❓ I can't see my friend's progress
Make sure you're both connected as friends (not just a pending request). If you recently became friends, try refreshing the app by pulling down on any screen.
❓ A challenge link isn't working
Make sure you have FitRival installed first. If you clicked the link before installing, download the app, then click the link again. If you're already logged in and it's not working, try copying the link and pasting it in your browser.
❓ The app is crashing or not loading
1. Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store/Play Store
2. Try force quitting and reopening the app
3. Restart your device
4. If issues persist, contact support below
